Tandem, the perfect balance for your household. Tandem organises household chores for a couple or a small family: who takes the bins out this week, who does the shopping, when to change the sheets… without having to ask again.

★ Today at a glance
Overdue tasks, today's and this week's, checked off with one tap. The weekly split shows who does what, with no ranking or competition.

★ Natural-language quick add
Type "Take the bins out tomorrow 7pm Grace": date, time and person are recognised automatically.

★ Repeats and taking turns
Every day, weekdays, every week, every other week, every month, last day of the month… and above all who takes care of it: always the same person, together, taking turns, a custom rotation or depending on the day. The preview shows the next dates and who is responsible for each.

★ Lists and subtasks
A shopping list or the steps of a task, checked off as you go.

★ Calendar
Month and day views; long-press to move a task to another day.

★ Reminders and notifications
A reminder before each scheduled task, and a notification when your partner gives you a task.

★ "Today" widget
Today's tasks on your home screen, checked off without opening the app.

★ Works offline
Check off and add tasks without a connection: everything syncs when you're back online.

★ Shared calendar (optional)
Dated tasks can be published to a shared Google calendar for the household.

★ Also on the web
The same agenda from a computer, with a single account.

Privacy first: no ads, no data resale, export and delete your account at any time. Dark mode, English and French, TalkBack support.

An Tandem account is required (sign up on the website, then invite your partner to the household).
